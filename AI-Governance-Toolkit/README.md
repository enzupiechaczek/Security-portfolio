# AI Governance Toolkit

## Purpose
This repository demonstrates a practical approach to maintaining visibility and control over AI systems across an organisation — covering inventory, risk classification, ownership, and review cycles, mapped to NIST AI RMF principles and ISO/IEC 42001 concepts. Built to reflect how AI governance can move from policy language into an operational, auditable process.

## Contents
- **AI_System_Register.csv**: Example inventory of AI systems with ownership, risk classification, data sensitivity, and approval status.
- **AI_Risk_Scoring_Methodology.md**: A 5x5 likelihood/impact scoring approach for AI-specific risks (bias, hallucination, data leakage, model drift, explainability).
- **AI_Policy_Template.md**: A concise AI use policy covering governance structure, approval workflow, and human oversight requirements.
- **Framework_Mapping.md**: Maps toolkit elements to NIST AI RMF functions and ISO/IEC 42001 clauses.
- **AI_Governance_Workflow.png**: Visual overview of the AI governance lifecycle: intake, risk assessment, approval, and monitoring.

## Context
Built to explore how organisations can operationalise AI governance in practice — particularly relevant as AI adoption accelerates faster than most governance structures can keep pace with. This reflects an interest in bridging technical AI literacy with structured, auditable governance discipline.
