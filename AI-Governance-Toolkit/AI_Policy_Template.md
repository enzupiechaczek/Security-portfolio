# AI Use Policy Template

## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes the principles and controls governing the responsible use, deployment, and monitoring of Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems within the organisation.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all AI systems, including third-party and internally developed models, used to process organisational or customer data.

## 3. Governance Structure
- **AI System Owner**: Accountable for the system's compliance, risk posture, and lifecycle management.
- **AI Governance Forum**: Reviews new AI use cases, approves deployment, and monitors ongoing risk.
- **Head of Information Security**: Provides oversight on security and data protection controls.

## 4. Approval Workflow
1. **Intake**: New AI use case submitted with system description, data flows, and intended purpose.
2. **Risk Assessment**: Evaluated against the AI Risk Scoring Methodology.
3. **Review**: AI Governance Forum reviews findings and either approves, approves with conditions, or rejects.
4. **Deployment**: Approved systems added to the AI System Register with assigned owner and review date.
5. **Monitoring**: Ongoing performance, drift, and incident monitoring per defined review cadence.

## 5. Human Oversight Requirements
- High-risk systems require human-in-the-loop review before any decision is actioned.
- Medium-risk systems require periodic human validation of outputs.
- Low-risk systems may operate with minimal oversight, subject to periodic audit.

## 6. Data Protection
All AI systems processing personal information must undergo a Privacy Impact Assessment prior to approval, in line with applicable data protection legislation (e.g., POPIA).

## 7. Review Cycle
All AI systems are subject to review at least every 6 months, or sooner if performance issues, incidents, or regulatory changes occur.
